01 // the trust stack

How you make an LLM agent provable

METHODS

What data may claim

Decomposition rules, composition-effect guards, causal-honesty constraints — a formal grammar for what an analysis is allowed to say from the data it saw.

VALIDATORS

Enforced at runtime

Deterministic Python that checks every number, filter, and claim in a generated output against source data — before anything reaches a client.

EVALUATION

Who checks the checkers

Mutation testing and golden regression suites: seed known defects into outputs, confirm the validators catch them. Trust, earned recursively.

This is the empty seat in the market: text-to-SQL and AI-analyst products can't certify their answers, and generic LLM-eval tooling doesn't understand analytical semantics. I build in the intersection.
